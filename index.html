02 / BACKGROUND

A curiosity for
what lies beneath.

My research explores the gap between encrypted content and observable behavior. I work on website fingerprinting, encrypted traffic analysis, and network protocol security at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Through measurement and learning-based methods, I aim to better understand information leakage in modern networked systems.
