About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IIE, CAS), and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), advised by Qingyun Liu (Professor-level Senior Engineer). I joined in 2021 as a master’s student and entered the integrated master’s–Ph.D. track in 2023. I expect to graduate in January 2027.

My research focuses on encrypted traffic analysis, website fingerprinting, and network protocol security, with recent work on the security of LLM agents. I combine protocol analysis, real-world measurement, and machine learning to study information leakage and security risks in networked systems. My first-author work includes NDSS 2027 (accepted), INFOCOM 2026, and WWW 2025 (Oral).

Next chapter. I will be joining Zhongguancun National Laboratory, in Academician Xiaochun Yun’s team.
